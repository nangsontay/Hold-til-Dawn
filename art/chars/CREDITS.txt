Character sprites in this folder
================================
scout, healer, engineer (CHR-01..03), grunt, spitter, wrecker (CHR-05..07):
original 32x32 pixel art (Wrecker 40x40) for Hold 'til Dawn, following the GDD style guide
(palette, dark outline, 8 directions: right, down, left, up and the four 3/4 diagonals (the front or
back silhouette with the face turned), left mirrored; idle 2 frames + walk 4 frames with a 1 px body bounce).
Each frame sits in a cell with 8 px of room around the body (48x48, Wrecker 56x56). After idle and walk:
4 frames per attack (players: bare hands, stone spear, metal blade, hammer, bow; Grunt claw rake,
Spitter spit, Wrecker ground smash), then 4 knock-down frames (hit, knees give, falling, lying with the
eyes shut), all made by tools/PixelPoses.cs; chars.json "anims" lists [first column, frames, fps].

Made by tools/PixelChars.cs: every sprite is written there as a pixel map (one letter per pixel);
the script adds the outline, the ground shadow and the walk/idle frames, and writes these PNGs.
No third-party asset and no AI image generator was used.

AI disclosure: the pixel maps and the script were written by an AI coding assistant (Claude)
from the team's GDD; declare them in GDD Appendix B as AI-made assets. The PNGs are plain
1x pixel art: the team can repaint them in Aseprite / Piskel and the game uses the edited files.

Bosses (boss_*.png, bosses.json)
--------------------------------
juggernaut, broodmother, bio_cannon, burrower, gatebreaker, phantom, ironhide (AGT-05..11):
original 48x48 pixel art, same sheet layout as above (8 direction rows; idle 2 + walk 4,
attack 4: wind-up, slam, impact, recover; knocked down 4).
Each is the NOX brute with the part that shows its move (horns, egg sacs, spore pods, claws,
ram fist, hood and cloak, front plates). Made by tools/PixelBosses.cs from shaded shapes;
same AI disclosure as above.

Harvesting extension: tools/PixelGatherPoses.cs adds 6 frames per action in all 8 directions
for Scout, Healer and Engineer, using their own body, clothes, gear and skin palette.
Columns 30-35: wood axe, 36-41: stone/titanium pickaxe, 42-47: hand-picking herbs.
Original idle, walk, attack and knocked-down cells keep their positions and pixels.
These original code-drawn additions were made with Codex; no third-party images were used.

Enemy readability redesign
-------------------------
Native pixel generator tools/PixelEnemyDesigns.cs supplies separate palettes and silhouettes:
Grunt: rust orange, bone crest and exposed ribs; Spitter: leaf green, long neck and lime acid sacs;
Wrecker: slate blue, squared stone shoulders and a cracked chest plate.
All eight facings include idle, walking, attack and fallen frames.
Bosses: rust/bone Juggernaut; coral/cream Broodmother with six insect legs;
forest/lime Bio-Cannon with spore chimneys; ochre/ivory Burrower with back ridges;
bronze/verdigris Gatebreaker with a square ram; ice/cyan Phantom with pointed hood and ribbons;
blue/steel Ironhide with angular armour fins. Bosses are displayed at 3x instead of 2x
(1.5 times their previous visual size), including attacks and corpses.
Review: tools/EnemyDesignPreview.tscn (headless: validates 1,120 sprite cells;
graphical: exercises eight facings, attacks and corpses and saves review screenshots under .tmp).
