Nature sprites in this folder
============================
Harvestable nature: pine_0..5 (living outer forest), rock_0..4 (outcrops), crag_0..2 (rock spires of the ridges),
reed_0..2 (along the streams), shrub_0..2 (near the base),
grass_0..3, grass_dry_0..1 and pebble_0..3 (ground scatter).
Resource nodes: node_wood_0..2 (fir with an axe notch), node_stone_0..1 (pale quarry boulder),
node_herb_0..1 (flowering herb), node_titanium_0..1 (dark rock with blue/violet crystals), and the remains
stump_0, rubble_0, herb_cut_0, ore_left_0. Living scenery resources also have four damage variants and an
empty variant. Dead tree sprites are no longer indexed or placed in the game.
ground.png: 256 x 256 tileable ground texture (8 x 8 tiles), near-white so the game tints it with the
day-grass and outer-zone colours.
index.json: {name: [anchor_x, anchor_y, solid_radius_px]}; the anchor is the pixel on the ground point.

Original pixel art for Hold 'til Dawn, drawn by tools/PixelNature.cs (1 px = 1 game px, 32 px tile,
GDD palette, light from the upper left, dark outline, soft shadow to the lower right). No third-party
asset and no AI image generator was used. (This replaces the earlier sprites rendered from the
Quaternius Stylized Nature MegaKit.)

Harvest stages are drawn by tools/PixelHarvestDamage.cs, revised with OpenAI Codex: trees lose lower boughs
and whole fronds above deepening axe cuts, rocks lose large angular chunks with fresh fracture faces,
and herbs lose leaf fans and flowers down to cut stems. Garden rows empty while their containers remain.

AI disclosure: the script was written by an AI coding assistant (Claude) from the team's GDD; declare
these sprites in GDD Appendix B as AI-made assets. The PNGs can be repainted by hand (Aseprite,
Piskel, ...): keep the file name and size, or update the anchor in index.json.
