Island landmark pixel art for Hold 'til Dawn
===========================================
The original landmarks are drawn by tools/PixelLandmarks.cs, after the project's
Hold 'til Dawn Landmark Atlas. The additional 27 set-dressing sprites are drawn
by tools/PixelLandmarkDetails.cs with the same palettes, canvas and pixel scale.
Both are partial files of PixelNature. Regenerate with:

godot --headless --path . tools/PixelNature.tscn -- --landmarks-only

Original project art, generated by deterministic C# drawing code. The additional
code and designs were produced with the OpenAI Codex coding assistant. No external
asset pack or image-generation service was used for these additional sprites.
The Atlas concept references remain in ref/ and are excluded from Godot import.

One art pixel is one world pixel; a tile is 32 pixels. index.json stores each
sprite's [anchor_x, anchor_y, solid_radius_px]. Large composite structures use
additional footprint circles in WorldLandmarks.cs / WorldLandmarkDetails.cs.
Scenery machinery and supplies are decorative, not interactive inventory items.

Expedition update: survey tent, research console, broken cart, quarantine sign,
cargo stack and water pump use native pixel geometry in tools/PixelExpeditionProps.cs,
with the project palettes and outline pipeline. No external images.
