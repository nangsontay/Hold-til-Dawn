Item icons in this folder
=========================
One 20 x 20 icon per inventory item, named by its item id: wood, stone, herb, metal, bandage, arrow,
stone_spear, metal_blade, starter_bow, bow, hammer, light_armour, stone_armour, metal_armour
(ITM-02..13). Shown in the hotbar, inventory and storage (2x), the resource panel and on dropped items (1x).

Original pixel art for Hold 'til Dawn, drawn by tools/PixelItems.cs from pixel maps (one letter per
pixel, GDD palette, light from the upper left, 1 px dark outline). No third-party asset and no AI
image generator was used.

AI disclosure: the pixel maps and the script were written by an AI coding assistant (Claude)
from the team's GDD; declare them in GDD Appendix B as AI-made assets. The PNGs can be repainted
by hand (Aseprite, Piskel, ...): keep the file name and the game uses the edited file.

Tactical additions: long_spear, stun_maul, vine_trap, poison_flask, healing_flask,
haste_tonic, gathering_kit, explosive_arrow, repair_kit, plus the explosive arrow
projectile in art/fx. Original pixel maps in tools/PixelTacticalItems.cs, generated
with the existing PixelItems outline and palette pipeline. Authored with Codex;
no third-party assets or image generator. Regenerate via tools/PixelItems.tscn.
